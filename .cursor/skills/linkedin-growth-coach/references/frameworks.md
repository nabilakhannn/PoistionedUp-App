# Reference: frameworks and numbers (operator playbook)

Use when the user needs the full cheat sheet. Paired with the main `SKILL.md`.

## Time allocation (ideal weekly focus)

| Bucket | Approx % | Purpose |
|--------|----------|---------|
| DM conversations | 37% | Pipeline and clients; most people under-index here |
| Posting content | 25% | Authority and reach |
| Prospecting / new connections | 12% | Fill the top of funnel |
| Commenting | 25% | Visibility and reciprocity |

If someone is 50% posting and 0% DMs, the fix is rebalancing, not a new "strategy."

## Daily engagement block (30 to 45 minutes, non-negotiable)

1. **First ~10 minutes:** Engage on your own post (reply, keep thread alive).
2. **Next ~20 to 25 minutes:** External commenting (feed + intentional targets).
3. **Final ~5 to 10 minutes:** DMs to people who showed intent (commented, viewed profile, connection request, etc.).

Posting without being present after is **post-and-ghost**; treat it as almost as bad as not posting.

### First publish window (acute)

Right after a post goes live, the **first ~60 to 90 minutes** matter most for replies to early commenters, planned **plus-three** author comments, and a burst of targeted external comments. Social proof and thread life compound early; do not schedule a post and disappear for half a day.

### Split schedule (when one block is impossible)

- **Before post:** 10 to 15 minutes comments (warm up feed + targets).
- **Post.**
- **After post:** 10 to 15 minutes on people who commented on you + reciprocal engagement on ICP posts.

Always protect **at least ~30 minutes** around publish time when possible.

## Thirty-day trackers (gym metaphor)

Track like reps. Without a sheet, people lie to themselves about effort.

| Tracker | Primary job |
|---------|-------------|
| Posting | Momentum and consistency |
| Comments | Visibility and authority |
| Outreach / DMs | Lead gen and compounding pipeline |

Optional: simple CRM list for conversations started and follow-ups.

## Toxic creator lifecycle (name it when coaching)

Common path: start strong, life interrupts, momentum drops, second-guess content, blame algorithm, chase new strategy, quit. **Antidote:** trackers + fixed engagement block + one strategy long enough to gather data.

## Seven habits (attention + clients)

Use as a diagnostic checklist when someone "does everything" but has no pipeline.

1. **Positioning evolves:** Reframe offer and language as the market saturates; same expertise, sharper angle.
2. **Nose for nos:** Rejection is inventory; law of averages; volume of quality asks.
3. **DMs weighted over feed fame:** Founder game first (pipeline, clients). Virality is a tool, not the scoreboard.
4. **Do not confuse the audience:** Profile behaves like a prompt; pillars reinforce signal; avoid shiny-object posts that break bingeability.
5. **Offer on the calendar:** Mention how you help **at least weekly**; new followers do not read your old Featured section.
6. **Vary content personality:** Rotate educate / story / lead magnet (or equivalent) so the brand is not one-note.
7. **Give before expecting return:** Pick a small list of peers; engage them **several days in a row**; swap out non-reciprocators over time.

## Twenty-percent levers (high leverage tactics)

- **Sniper engagement:** Active people with credibility in your space; open with specific observation, not flattery templates.
- **Skill endorsements:** Lightweight touch that triggers notification; bridge to thoughtful comment or DM when appropriate.
- **Comment quality:** Specific, human reactions beat generic praise that reads like AI.
- **Repeatable formats:** Same structure, new topic (POV, weekly roundup, "highlights you missed," craft demo that maps to your service).
- **Named frameworks:** Coin repeatable language for your method so memory and referrals stick.

## Influencer vs operator frame

If scale goal is revenue: prioritize **pipeline obsession** over notification obsession. You can add fame later; many burn reach because the offer and DM system were never built.

## Three ICP engagement tiers

- **Tier 1:** Direct ICP (people who could buy).
- **Tier 2:** One step outside niche where ICP hides (e.g. active commenters on large creators your buyers follow).
- **Tier 3:** Peer creators in adjacent spaces (reciprocity, community, learning).

## DM conversation patterns

- **Voice notes (optional):** For high-value ICP where tone and warmth matter, a **short** personalized voice note can stand out versus text-only spam. Use sparingly; still anchor in something you actually read or saw.
- **Soft diagnostic questions** can work when rooted in real observation ("how is X going for you?" after you saw their content pattern) so they self-articulate pain.
- **Bad:** "I'd love to network," generic bump, recycled Loom with no personalization.
- **Good:** Proof you looked, name likely pain, low-pressure invite to chat.

## Content calendar rhythm (examples)

- **Educate / story / lead magnet** loop so the feed is not one-dimensional.
- **Writing hierarchy (weekday map):** Assign a post *type* per day (e.g. platitude, step-by-step, story, video, promo) to cut decision fatigue. User picks topics only.

## Broad / narrow / niche (content and topics)

- **Broad:** Large curiosity, industry-level or identity hook; reach and top of funnel.
- **Narrow:** Domain expertise, problem-aware reader, still not the full offer.
- **Niche:** Offer-level, hand-raisers, proof, spots, case study with CTA.

Same *format* (story, carousel, infographic) can run at broad, narrow, or niche by changing the *topic layer*.

## Cadence: avoid "whole week themed"

Do **not** make every post in a week about one subtopic only. **Vary angle and post type day to day** while staying inside your pillars. Theme weeks are brittle for testing.

## Four-three-two-one (example cadence)

Rough pattern: **four** posts per week across **three** content types: educational, personal or founder-led, lead gen or offer-adjacent. Adjust numbers to user's reality.

## Hook rules (feed behavior)

- Optimize for "see more" / dwell: first lines must **not** resolve the whole arc.
- **Growing accounts:** question hooks often underperform **bold statements** because you want to be read as the expert with a point of view, not a poll.
- **Credential dumps:** move proof to listicles or later lines; do not block the hook.

## One-post-one-job

Do not stack in a single post: full saga + full framework + hard sell. Split across posts or use **plus-three** first comments for overflow.

## Profile and creative guardrails

- Headline, banner, featured: **one coherent promise**.
- Remove ad cues: logo watermarks, unreadable screenshots, stock when a human photo would convert.
- Featured: promise a **need** ("learn X to get Y"), not "subscribe" with no reason.
- **Trust friction (remote / global sellers):** add legitimate proof: client geographies, logos or faces if real, concrete outcomes without exaggeration.

## Lead magnets

- Hook = **pain + payoff** in plain language; avoid internal jargon in the title.
- Visual: **preview the asset** (gif, doc snippet). User must see what is inside.
- Title the magnet from **their outcome**, not your process name.

## Carousels and infographics

- **Cover slide** must telegraph payoff (graph, money, bold headline tied to title).
- Prefer **taller / readable** layouts over cramped squares; high contrast; simple background (often white or black) so text wins.
- When **testing formats**, change **one variable** at a time (e.g. same layout, new topic).

## Tweet-style visuals on LinkedIn

Many feeds associate **black / white tweet-like** layouts with "good thread content." If you adopt the format, keep variables stable so recognition compounds.

## Metrics stance

- Impressions/reach: noisy; do not spiral on them alone.
- Prefer: likes, comments, DMs, calls, follower quality.
- **Polarizing / rant** posts: treat debate and comments as data about distribution, not as proof the impression number is "true."
- Optional: **accountability trio** comparing public likes on today's post.

## Length guidance

Skimmable beats exhaustive. Past ~800 to 1200 words without strong reason: split posts or cut. Reintroduction posts especially: intro, credibility list, why follow, PS.

## Repurpose rules

- Repurpose **angles**, not only duplicate copy.
- If nobody saw it: repost with stronger hook/visual soon; tweak hook numbers or first line.

## Language consistency (bilingual)

Default: **one primary feed language.** Mixing adds translation friction. Exception: **visual-first** post where creative carries meaning; then test cross-language deliberately, not random toggling.

## Platform mechanics (pragmatic)

- **Heavy tagging** of many people who do not engage can look like spam to systems and humans. Tag sparingly with intent.
- **Linking older posts** ("in case you missed it") often underperforms; rewrite and repackage instead of recycling low-proof links.
- **Large edits after publish:** avoid; may reset distribution behavior. Fix in comments or new post when possible.
- **Role / milestone announcements:** native announcement formats can widen reach when used with a real story and photo.

## Employer + personal brand split

When the profile serves two masters: assign slots (example: 4 posts/week: one employer-adjacent, one adjacent market story, one personal founder story, one deep educational on future wedge). Adjust ratios to politics and goals.

## Stigma and sensitive niches

If buyers will not comment publicly on core pain: lead through **adjacent pains** (habits, stress, money leak, performance, identity) and **stories** that create safety; save direct CTA for DMs or assets.

## Offer validation before build

**Waitlist / talk before heavy build:** describe outcome, collect demand, ship to warm demand. Useful when debating "build it first" vs "sell it first."

## Give-the-what vs sell-the-how

Feed can teach **high level** and frameworks; full nuance lives in delivery. Do not turn every story into a lead magnet pitch; that reads inauthentic. Sequence: attention and trust posts, then dedicated conversion posts.

## Viral post follow-up

If a post blew up on **education:** next post often should build **authority and person** (why you, why now), not another identical edu piece. **DM** only **ICP** engagers; skip mass thank-yous.

## Merged or shifting ICP

Publish **intersection** content: why the two worlds connect, story of the pivot, educational splits that speak to both until data shows where to lean.

## Trend and attention bridges

If the offer is narrow (ops, data, recruiting): top of funnel hooks may still borrow **macro attention** (AI, speed, money, risk) then bridge to niche proof.

## Connection inbox hygiene

Large pending queue hurts momentum. Batch clear or outsource with clear ICP rules.

## Nurture tempo

Short cycles of touchpoints beat one mega-pitch. Multiple light DMs and value drops across days/weeks; qualify before long calls.

## Questions to ask when information is missing

- Who pays you, in one sentence?
- What worked once, even small?
- DMs sent last 7 days (real count)?
- Offer mentioned how many times in 14 days?
- What are you avoiding saying because it feels "salesy"?
