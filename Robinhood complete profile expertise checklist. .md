Use this one‑page audit any time you refresh your profile. Tick each box only when the item is **done and live**. The algorithm loves *coherence*—every section should point to the same niche story.

---

## **1\. Profile Photo** 

- [ ] High‑resolution, head‑and‑shoulders, clean background.  
- [ ] Match the dress code of recognised experts in your field.  
- [ ] Eyes level with the camera.  
- [ ] MAKE SURE PHOTO IS PUBLIC.

## **2\. Banner Image** 

- [ ] One bold line: **who you help \+ outcome** (e.g., “I help SaaS founders 3× demo calls”).  
- [ ] Branded colour or niche‑specific visual; skip generic stock.  
- [ ] Arrow or line of sight aimed at the **Follow** button.  
- [ ] Slippery flow (optional)

## **3\. Headline** 

- [ ] Formula: *Role* \+ *Niche* \+ *Transformation* \+ proof number. *Example:* “B2B SaaS Ghostwriter | Turns cold posts into 7‑figure pipeline”.  
- [ ] Front‑load your niche keyword for search; keep ≤ 220 characters.

## **4\. Name, Pronunciation & Pronouns** 

- [ ] First \+ Last only. Credential (e.g., CFA NOT OKAY) no hashtags.  
- [ ] Record the name‑pronunciation—boosts connection \+ accessibility.  
- [ ] Pronouns optional but can humanise and widen search filters.

## **5\. Contact & Custom URL** 

- [ ] Custom URL: **/in/firstname‑lastname‑niche** if available.  
- [ ] Visible email, Calendly, WhatsApp/Signal link.

## **6\. About Section** 

- [ ] First 300 chars repeat your niche keyword \+ clear promise.  
- [ ] Layout: **Hook →Hell→Heaven→  Credibility → Method → Proof → CTA**.  
- [ ] Weave past careers into a single through‑line that lands on today’s expertise.

## **7\. Featured Section** 

- [ ] TOP offer and Bottom Offer Combo  
- [ ] Strong thumbnails with verb cues (“Download”, “Watch”).  
- [ ] Refresh quarterly—timestamp freshness \= relevance boost.

## **8\. Experience, Projects & Courses** 

- [ ] **Current role title** must match the keyword you post about; LinkedIn cross‑checks recency.  
- [ ] Bullet 1 = result metric; Bullet 2 = method; Bullet 3 = who benefited.  
- [ ] Add *Projects* & *Courses* that reinforce your speciality (e.g., “HubSpot RevOps Build 2024”).  
- [ ] Past roles: rewrite headlines so each feeds the same narrative (e.g., “Project Manager → Systems thinker for sales teams”).

## **9\. Skills & Endorsements** 

- [ ] Pin the top 3 skills—exact match to your headline.  
- [ ] **Endorsements** on each pinned skill (ask credible peers/clients).  
- [ ] Remove unrelated legacy skills; irrelevant noise dilutes mastery.

## **10\. Recommendations** 

- [ ] Minimum 4: two clients, one peer, one former leader.  
- [ ] First sentence must carry the transformation keyword (“She 3× our inbound leads…”).  
- [ ] Aim for recommenders recognised in your target industry—LinkedIn weighs their authority.

## **11\. Verification & Credentials** 

- [ ] Apply for the **Verified** badge (company email or ID); verified pros see broader distribution.  
- [ ] List publications, patents, honours/awards—anything that signals mastery.

## **12\. Additional Sections** 

- [ ] **Volunteer Experience & Causes**—humanises \+ keyword juice.  
- [ ] **Languages & Organisations**—more vectors for discovery.  
- [ ] **Test Scores** or **Licences/Certs** if relevant to niche.

## **13\. Activity & Original Thought Hygiene** 

- [ ] Post at least **once a week** with fresh POV; LinkedIn tracks likelihood of “adding original thoughts.”  
- [ ] Comment daily on 5 industry experts; mirror their vocabulary.

## **14\. Publish a Niche Newsletter** 

- [ ] Title \= niche keyword \+ promised insight (e.g., “RevOps Weekly Wins”).  
- [ ] Consistent cadence (monthly or bi‑weekly). Newsletters are indexed and surface you as subject‑matter expert.

## **15\. Keyword Consistency Check** 

- [ ] Ctrl‑F your main keyword: should appear in Photo alt‑text, Headline, About, Experience, Skills.  
- [ ] Keep density \~1–2 %; avoid stuffing.

## **16\. Proof Media** 

- [ ] Attach PDFs/images in Experience showing before‑after graphs, press clippings, or case‑study teasers.  
- [ ] Alt‑text describes the win (“Graph: 212 % traffic lift for fintech client”).

## **17\. Services page** 

- [ ] Make sure relevant services are listed so that you are indexed in search.   
- [ ] Add review images on the services page.
