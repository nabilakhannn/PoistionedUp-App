## 1. CREATOR / INFLUENCER PROFILE

**Who they are:** Solo practitioners who sell expertise as a service. Coaches (life, business, executive, health), consultants (marketing, ops, HR, finance), and B2B service providers (designers, fractional CMOs, accountants) who need personal brand visibility to attract clients.

**They are NOT influencers in the traditional sense.** They don't want to "be famous." They want to be visible enough that ideal clients find them instead of the other way around. The distinction matters for your product messaging: this isn't about vanity metrics. It's about client acquisition through content.

**Creator maturity levels (where your ICP sits):**

| Level | Description | Content Behavior | PositionedUp Fit |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Level 0 | No content presence | Doesn't post at all, relies on referrals | Too early, not ready to pay |
| Level 1 | Sporadic poster | Posts 1-3x/month when motivated, no strategy | SWEET SPOT, highest pain |
| Level 2 | Inconsistent creator | Posts weekly-ish, tried AI tools, gets generic output | SWEET SPOT, ready to upgrade |
| Level 3 | Active creator | Posts 3-5x/week, has some systems, wants to scale quality | Good fit, power user |
| Level 4 | Content machine | Daily posting, team support, established brand | Likely has existing tools |

**Your ICP sits at Level 1-2.** They know they should create content. They've tried. They can't maintain consistency because every piece feels like starting from scratch.

## 3. BEHAVIOUR ANALYSIS

### Content Creation Behavior
- Most coaches know content matters: 74% of consumers rely on social media to guide purchasing decisions
- But coaches juggle coaching, admin, marketing, and business development simultaneously
- Average coach manages 12 active clients at a time
- Average coaching hours: 11.6 hours/week just on delivery
- Content creation typically gets pushed to "when I have time" which means evenings and weekends

### Tool Usage Behavior
- 69.1% of marketers have integrated AI into operations (HubSpot)
- BUT most coaches have only "dabbled" with ChatGPT for captions or subject lines
- They download templates, try prompts, get overwhelmed, abandon
- 84% deliver services via video/audio platforms (Zoom, Teams) so they're already tech-comfortable, just not content-tech-comfortable

### Buying Behavior
- 81% of coaches get new clients through personal referrals (their current strategy)
- They buy tools based on peer recommendations, not ads
- They prefer "done-with-you" over "done-for-you" or pure DIY
- Price sensitivity is moderate: they'll pay $50-300/month for tools that directly impact revenue
- They cancel tools they don't use within 30 days

### Content Consumption Behavior
- YouTube is their primary learning platform (they watch coaching/business content)
- LinkedIn is their primary professional visibility platform
- They follow 5-10 thought leaders in their space and feel intimidated by their output quality
- They read newsletters (Morning Brew model) more than blogs
- Podcasts are big: 65% listen regularly to business/coaching podcasts

### Business Structure

The single biggest operational reality:

| Activity | Hours/Week | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Client coaching sessions | 11.6 | Core delivery |

| **Total** | **24-34** | Content gets whatever is left |

**Content gets 2-4 hours/week at best.** That's enough for maybe 1-2 posts if they're efficient. It's nowhere near enough for multi-platform consistency.

## 5. PAIN POINTS (ranked by severity)

### Tier 1: Existential Pains (keep them up at night)
1. **"I'm invisible online while competitors with WORSE results grow faster."** This is the core wound. They have real expertise and client results but can't translate it into digital visibility.
2. **"I know I need to create content but I can't stay consistent."** They start strong every January and quit by February. Every new tool promises to fix this.
3. **"AI makes me sound generic. My clients would know it's not me."** They've tried ChatGPT. The output sounds like every other coach. Their unique voice, stories, and perspective get lost.

### Tier 2: Operational Pains (daily frustrations)
4. **"I stare at a blank screen for 45 minutes and produce one mediocre post."** The content creation process itself is painful, not just the output.
5. **"I don't know what to post about. I've said everything I know."** Content ideation drought. They feel like they've exhausted their topics.
6. **"I can't figure out what works. I post and get nothing. Then randomly something hits."** No performance feedback loop. No pattern recognition.
7. **"Admin eats my coaching time."** Content creation is just one more admin task competing for limited hours.

### Tier 3: Strategic Pains (longer-term anxieties)
8. **"I don't have a brand. I have a name and a logo."** They sense they need positioning but can't articulate what makes them different.
9. **"I'm falling behind on AI and I don't know where to start."** AI anxiety. They know it's important but it's overwhelming.
10. **"Referrals are drying up and I don't have a pipeline."** The referral-only model is fragile. One bad quarter away from crisis.

## 13. DECISION MAKERS

This is almost always a **single decision maker** (the coach/consultant themselves). Key nuances:

- **Solo decision:** 85%+ of purchases. They see it, they try it, they buy or don't.
- **Spouse/partner influence:** At the $97+ price point, some will "discuss with spouse." Not a formal approval but a gut check.
- **Peer influence:** MASSIVE. Coaches buy what other coaches recommend. One influential coach endorsing your tool is worth $50K in ads.
- **Accountant/bookkeeper flag:** At the $200+ tier, they may notice and ask "what's this subscription?"

**Decision timeline:**
- See the product: Day 0
- Free trial / quick demo: Day 0-3
- First content generated: Day 1-7 (this is the critical moment)
- Purchase decision: Day 7-14
- Stick or cancel: Day 30
